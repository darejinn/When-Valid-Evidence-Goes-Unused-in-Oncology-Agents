\section*{Numbering and materials}
For the archival body, PDF page equals original printed page minus eight.
The addendum uses printed pages S1 onward. References to main sections and
figures refer to the submitted manuscript; compact and archival table numbering
can differ.

The repository includes the unchanged original supplement in \texttt{archive/},
the addendum PDF, its LaTeX source, the paired outcome-label CSV, and analysis/build
scripts. Section S1 explains how the studies relate to one another; Section S6
describes how to reproduce the paired counts and build the PDFs.

The repository excludes licensed candidate text, full response transcripts, and
private reader forms. The public outcome labels allow readers to recompute
counts, but independently regrading responses would require the underlying
records and outputs. We have not completed a census of common-prompt
explanations or new independent source validation for this update.
