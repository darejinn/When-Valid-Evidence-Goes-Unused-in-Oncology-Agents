\section*{Numbering and materials}
For the archival body, PDF page equals original printed page minus eight.
The addendum uses printed pages S1 onward. References to main sections and
figures refer to the submitted manuscript; compact and archival table numbering
can differ.

The repository includes the unchanged original supplement in \texttt{archive/},
the addendum PDF, its LaTeX source, the paired outcome-label CSV, and analysis/build
scripts. Historical references within the archival body to internal versions,
code files, a larger delivery package, or a companion report identify provenance;
they do not imply that all those materials are included here.

Licensed candidate text, full response transcripts, and private reader forms are
not redistributed. Public outcome labels support reaggregation rather than
independent response regrading. No completed common-prompt explanation census or
new independent source validation is claimed in this update.
